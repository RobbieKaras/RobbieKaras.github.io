## Why this site exists

I write a log entry at the end of most coding sessions: the technical facts of
what changed, and then my own reasoning for why. The reasoning half is the
point. It's easy to finish a project and not be able to explain any of the
decisions in it a month later, and I'd rather not be in that position when
someone asks.
